# YouTube A-B Loop

Pick any part of a YouTube video and replay it on repeat. Set a start (A), set an end (B), and enjoy the loop.

No downloads. No edits to the video. Just a small panel on the YouTube player.

---

## Install

1. Unzip `yt-ab-loop.zip`. You get a folder called `yt-ab-loop`.
2. Open Chrome and go to `chrome://extensions`.
3. Turn on **Developer mode** (top right).
4. Click **Load unpacked**.
5. Select the `yt-ab-loop` folder (the one that has `manifest.json` inside) and click **Select**.
6. Make sure the extension toggle is on.

Done. Nothing else to set up.

---

## How to use

1. Open any video on youtube.com (reload the tab if it was already open).
2. Play the video and move your mouse over the player. The panel appears at the bottom-left.
3. At the moment you want the loop to start, click **Set A**.
4. At the moment you want the loop to end, click **Set B**.
5. The video jumps to A and keeps looping from A to B.

### The panel

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Set A** | Saves the current time as the loop start |
| **Set B** | Saves the current time as the loop end |
| **A / B times** | Shows your saved points |
| **Loop: ON / OFF** | Turn the loop on or off without losing your points |
| **Reset** | Clears A and B so you can pick new ones |

### Good to know

- Tip: pause the video, scrub to the exact spot, then click Set A or Set B for a precise point.
- If you set B before A, the two points swap automatically.
- Points reset when you open a different video.
- The loop pauses during ads and continues after.
- The panel hides with the YouTube controls and shows again when you move the mouse.

---

## Update

Replace the files in your `yt-ab-loop` folder, click the reload icon on the extension card in `chrome://extensions`, then reload your YouTube tab.

## Remove

Go to `chrome://extensions` and click **Remove** on YouTube A-B Loop.
